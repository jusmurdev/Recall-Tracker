# Recall Tracker

Know the moment something you buy, eat, or feed your family gets recalled.

Recall Tracker is a mobile app for the US that watches every recall the FDA, USDA and CPSC
publish and tells you only about the ones that matter to you: the brands in your pantry, the
items on your receipt, the restaurants you eat at.

## What it does

**Free**
- A plain-English feed of every US food, drug, cosmetic, pet food and household product recall,
  rated Serious, Moderate or Minor, with "what to do" steps and the lot codes to check.
- Watch a brand, a barcode, or a whole category and get a push notification when it's recalled.
- Scan a product label or a barcode for an instant verdict. Scan a receipt and every line is
  checked at once. OCR runs on your phone; only text is sent.
- A map of restaurants near you with their health inspection grade and a safety rating.
- Alerts you can act on: "I handled it", "I don't have this", "wrong match". Quiet hours and a
  daily digest instead of a buzz per alert.
- Location-aware ranking using only your state, never your exact position.

**Premium**
- Track a restaurant: we research who supplies its kitchen and alert you when a supplier is
  recalled or its health grade drops, including a heads-up when you walk in.
- Connect grocery and delivery accounts so everything you buy is watched automatically.
- Photo identification for labels OCR can't read, and AI decoding of cryptic receipt lines.

## Where the data comes from

Public government sources only: the FDA's openFDA enforcement reports, the USDA Food Safety and
Inspection Service, the Consumer Product Safety Commission, and city health-department
inspection records (New York City and Chicago to start). One shared service pulls these a few
times a day so phones never query government servers directly.
